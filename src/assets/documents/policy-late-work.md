# Late Work & Deadline Extensions

Try your best to submit assignments on time. But don't panic if you need to submit an assignment late. **You may submit an assignment late without penalty by using the provided _slip day_ accommodations.** Otherwise, in the interest of fairness to all students, late work is not accepted and will receive 0 credit.

**Timely feedback and grading of your submission is not guaranteed if you submit using _any form_ of deadline extension accommodation.** Your grade/feedback may be delayed around 2 weeks. Future milestones are due as assigned regardless of whether you have received your previous milestone's feedback.

## Deadline Extensions

If using your slip days does not provide you with sufficient access to the course, let us know by requesting additional accommodations **2 business days** in advance of the deadline. Email <info4340@cornell.edu> and we will work with you to develop **reasonable accommodations** (i.e. a 24 or 48 hour extension). **Please do not reveal personal information in your request.** We respect your privacy as your future employers are also required to do.

As per university policy, **accommodations are not retroactive** (cannot be granted after the deadline has passed). Lastly, we are **unable** to provide accommodations for too much work in other classes, extracurricular activities, job interviews, or because ChatGPT misled you.
