# Inclusivity Statement

This course is designed to be inclusive and accessible to all students. If you have any concerns about the inclusivity of this course, let me know, and I will do my best to address your concerns.

I want you to feel like you belong in this class and are respected. Cornell University (as an institution) and I (as a human being and instructor of this course) are committed to full inclusion in education for all persons. If for any reason you feel that I have failed these goals, either let me know or [report it](https://diversity.cornell.edu/our-commitments/bias-reporting-cornell).
