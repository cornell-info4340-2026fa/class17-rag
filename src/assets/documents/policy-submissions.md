# Assignment Submission Policy

Handwritten work should be legible; no credit is provided for illegible submissions.

**Assignments should be _fully submitted (pushed to GitHub and submission form completed for that assignment)_ for credit.** If you fail to complete the entire submission procedure, including the submission form, you will receive 0 credit. Your assignment's submission time is the date and time you complete the submission form regardless of whether your completed the work at an earlier time. **No exceptions.**

Professionalism is a learning objective of this course. **You are responsible for checking and verifying that your work is complete, functional, and submitted to the correct assignment in the submission form.** No leniency is provided for forgetting to complete the submission form, submitting broken work, or submitting to the wrong assignment. You wouldn't submit work to a very important client that you didn't check first or give a client another client's deliverables. Use this class to practice this level of professionalism.
