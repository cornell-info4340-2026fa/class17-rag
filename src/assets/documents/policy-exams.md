# Exams

All exams in the course are oral exams held _in-person_ during scheduled times. Failure to schedule or attend an oral exam will result in a 0 for that exam.

## Exam Accommodations

Because exam dates are known in advance, all students are expected to take the exams at the scheduled times unless they have an accommodation. If you have an accommodation to take the exam on another date, you must notify us no later than 7 days prior to the exam.

## Makeup Exams

I understand that you may need to miss an exam for a legitimate reason (e.g., illness, emergency, religious observance, etc.). Provided timely notice is given, we will reschedule your oral exam.
