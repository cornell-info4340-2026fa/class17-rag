# Accommodations & Flexibility

This course is designed to be inclusive and accessible to all students.

## Academic Accommodations

Accommodations are provided in support of **equitable classroom _access_**.

Per [university policy](https://deanoffaculty.cornell.edu/policies-procedures/faculty-handbook/6-academic-policies-and-responsibilities/), this course provides the following accommodations:

- Disability Accommodations
- Religious-Observance Accommodations
- Title IX Accommodations
- Varsity Athlete Accommodations
- Medical Accommodations
- Military Service
- Other Accommodations

This course has built-in deadline (_slip days_) accommodations. **These accommodations are specifically intended to support _most_ student accommodation needs** (e.g. disability, religious observance, Title IX, athletic participation, military service, medical and mental health concerns, family emergencies, late enrollment, etc.)

If necessary, we will provide reasonable accommodations beyond the built-in accommodations. **Contact <info4340@cornell.edu> well in advance (at least 2 business days) of when you will need the accommodations.** The sooner, the better (per university policy, accommodations are not applied retroactively.) Requests for accommodations made within a few hours of a deadline (or after) are unlikely to receive a timely response and may not be able to be accommodated.

## Provided Accommodations

All students are provided with the following accommodations in this course.

These accommodations are specifically intended to support _most_ student accommodation needs and should only be used for legitimate reasons (e.g. disability, religious observance, Title IX, athletic participation, military service, medical and mental health concerns, family emergencies, late enrollment, etc.)

### Slip Day Accommodation

**You are provided with a total of _2 slip days_ for the entire semester.** **One** slip day permits you to submit an assignment (homework or project milestone only) up to 24 hours after the deadline (including weekends, holidays, and breaks) and receive full credit. You may not use slip days on group assignments.

**To use your slip days, simply submit your assignment after the original deadline, but before the assignment's _slip day deadline_.** Every 24 hours past the deadline will consume 1 slip day. (1 second passed the assignment's original deadline = 1 slip day.)

The slip day accommodation may not be combined with any other type of deadline extension accommodation. If you receive a deadline extension accommodation, you may not use your slip days to further extend the deadline.

### Resubmission Accommodation

**All assignments are graded _exactly_ once**. However, you are provided with **one** resubmission accommodation. A resubmission accommodation permits you to resubmit **one** assignment (project milestone or homework only; class preparation, exams, etc. excluded) for full credit one week after your grade for that assignment was returned to you. You may not resubmit group assignments.

Email <info4340@cornell.edu> to notify us that you wish to use your resubmission accommodation no later than 5 days after your grade was returned to you. We will reopen and extend that assignment's deadline in Canvas by one week (7 days / 168 hours; includes weekends, holidays, and breaks).

We will grade your resubmission from scratch. Your resubmission grade is final, regardless of whether it is higher than your original submission's grade.

No resubmissions, redos, or do-overs are accepted outside the provided resubmission accommodations.
